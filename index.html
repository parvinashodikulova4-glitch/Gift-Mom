A letter from the heart

To my precious mother...

My dearest Mom,

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, just for a moment. Maybe then you would understand how extraordinary you truly are.

You are one of the strongest women I have ever known. Life hasn't always been gentle with you, yet somehow, you keep going. Through every difficulty, every exhausting day, and every moment when you must miss home more than words can express, you continue to work, to care, and to smile. Even in a distant city, far away from the people you love, you carry so much responsibility on your shoulders. And you do it all with a strength that I sometimes don't know how to put into words.

But Mom, there is something I want you to remember...

You are living this life for the very first time, too. Before you became our mother, you were someone's little girl. You were once a little daughter, someone's precious princess, with your own dreams, wishes, and a whole lifetime waiting ahead of you.

Please don't forget that girl.

Please allow yourself to live, not just to work, sacrifice, and take care of everyone else. Laugh more. Travel. Discover beautiful places. Buy yourself flowers. Follow your dreams. Enjoy the little things. You deserve a life filled with happiness, not just a life spent making everyone else happy.

I know I am not always good at expressing my feelings. Sometimes I struggle to say how much I love you, and perhaps I don't show it as often as I should. But please never mistake my silence for a lack of love.

No matter how far apart we are, no matter where life takes us, I will always be by your side. You will always have me. My love for you has no limits, no conditions, and no distance.

And if life ever gets too heavy, I hope you remember that you don't always have to be strong. You are allowed to rest, to be vulnerable, to be taken care of, and simply to be happy.

I am so proud to be your daughter. And if I could choose my mother in a thousand different lifetimes, I would still choose you.

With endless love,

Your daughter, Parvina ♡

P.S. Mom, this little surprise is just a reminder that someone, somewhere, loves you more than words could ever explain.

A little gift, from me to you

For the little princess
who deserves the whole world.
